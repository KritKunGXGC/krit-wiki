## Summary
Tim Miller introduces the "FINVA" folder structure (Fleeting, In Progress, Notes, Views, Archives) for Obsidian. The system prioritizes folder-based workflow over organization, relying on MOCs, Bases, links, and effective note titles for knowledge management.

# How I use Folders in Obsidian

[Source](https://obsidian.rocks/how-i-use-folders-in-obsidian/) | By Tim Miller

## The Problem with Folders
Folders are binary and static, making it difficult to keep them organized as projects grow.

## The FINVA Folder Structure
Tim Miller uses a 5-folder system:
- 1 Fleeting
- 2 In Progress
- 3 Notes
- 4 Views
- 5 Archives

## Principles
- Use folders for *workflow*, not *organization*.
- Use MOCs, Bases, and effective note titles for organization.
- Rely on links to create relationships between notes.
