## Summary
ElevenLabs provides an integrated AI audio platform built on foundational research. Its capabilities are split into three main areas:

1. **ElevenCreative**: A comprehensive editor for generating realistic speech, music, and sound effects, alongside voice cloning and AI-powered video tools.
2. **ElevenAgents**: A platform for creating, deploying, and monitoring conversational voice agents for omnichannel customer experience.
3. **ElevenAPI**: A suite of powerful APIs for integrating text-to-speech, speech-to-text, and music generation into external applications.

The platform is designed for both creators seeking high-quality media generation and developers building scalable voice-driven applications.

## Overview
ElevenLabs is an AI voice generator and voice agents platform providing lifelike speech generation, voice cloning, and conversational agent deployment across multiple channels.

## Core Platforms

### ElevenCreative
Focuses on content creation:
- Ultra-realistic speech synthesis (70+ languages).
- Voice cloning and design.
- Music generation.
- Sound effects (SFX) generation.
- Image and video editing capabilities.

### ElevenAgents
Focuses on conversational agents:
- Configure, deploy, and monitor agents.
- Omnichannel support (phone, chat, email, WhatsApp).
- Analytics for success rates and CX metrics.
- Testing and guardrails for compliance.

## ElevenAPI
Provides robust APIs for developers:
- Text to Speech (Eleven Flash, Eleven Multilingual, Eleven v3).
- Speech to Text (Eleven Scribe).
- Music API.
