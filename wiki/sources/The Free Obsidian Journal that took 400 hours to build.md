# The Free Obsidian Journal that took 400 hours to build.

[Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a-beIdZBe6k) | By Focus Café

## Content Overview
This video discusses best practices for using plugins in Obsidian and highlights 6 essential, unobtrusive plugins.

## Plugins Mentioned
- **Sort and Permute Lines**: Easily sort and shuffle lists and headings.
- **Settings Search**: Adds a search box to the settings menu.
- **Recent Files**: Displays a list of recently opened files for easy navigation.
- **Tag Wrangler**: Bulk management of tags.
- **Paste Image Rename**: Automatically prompt to rename images when pasted.
- **Omnisearch**: Integrates quick switching with deep vault content search.
