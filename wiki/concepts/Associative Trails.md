# Associative Trails

**Associative Trails** represent the core navigation mechanism of the [[wiki/concepts/Memex|Memex]], conceptualized by [[wiki/entities/Vannevar Bush|Vannevar Bush]] in 1945. 

## Definition
Unlike traditional library cataloging systems (which are alphabetical or hierarchical), associative trails allow users to link any two items or pages together directly. By selecting a link, a user can instantly traverse from one piece of knowledge to another, mimicking the associative nature of the human brain.

## Legacy
Associative trails are the direct precursor to the modern **hyperlink** and form the backbone of the World Wide Web, network-based note-taking, and semantic knowledge bases.
