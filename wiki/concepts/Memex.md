# Memex

The **Memex** (a portmanteau of "memory" and "index") is a hypothetical proto-hypertext device first described by [[wiki/entities/Vannevar Bush|Vannevar Bush]] in his 1945 article **"As We May Think"**.

## Core Characteristics
- **Intimate Memory Supplement**: A personalized machine designed for storing an individual's books, records, and correspondence.
- **Speed & Flexibility**: Mechanized to allow rapid and highly flexible retrieval of stored information.
- **Associative Indexing**: Instead of relying on rigid, alphabetical, or hierarchical classification, it organizes information through [[wiki/concepts/Associative Trails|Associative Trails]].

## Historical Impact
The Memex is considered the conceptual forebear of the World Wide Web, personal wikis (like Obsidian), and modern LLM-driven knowledge management solutions.
