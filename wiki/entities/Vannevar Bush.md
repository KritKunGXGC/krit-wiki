# Vannevar Bush

Vannevar Bush (1890–1974) was an American engineer, inventor, and science administrator. During World War II, he headed the U.S. Office of Scientific Research and Development (OSRD), where he orchestrated military research and development.

## Contributions & Vision
He is best known in the field of information science for describing the [[wiki/concepts/Memex|Memex]], a hypothetical proto-hypertext system, in his landmark 1945 article **"As We May Think"** published in *The Atlantic Monthly*.

His visionary ideas on organizing knowledge and establishing [[wiki/concepts/Associative Trails|Associative Trails]] directly inspired future internet pioneers like Douglas Engelbart, Ted Nelson, and Tim Berners-Lee.
