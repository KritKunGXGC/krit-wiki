## General Claude prompt sequence for Excel freelancing jobs

Before I give you the exact prompts from the video, I also wanted to share the general prompt sequence I usually follow when I’m using Claude for this kind of work.

Because obviously, not every Excel job is going to be the exact same. One client might ask you to clean a contact list, another might ask you to remove certain records, another might want a messy database reorganized, and another might want a dashboard or summary sheet.

So instead of just uploading the file and saying “do this job,” I usually like to go step by step. First, I get Claude to understand the file. Then I get it to understand what the client is asking for. Then I make it break the job into smaller tasks. And only after that, I let it start making changes.

Also, before you do anything, ***always keep a safe copy of the original file***. Don’t only work on the client’s original file, because if something goes wrong, you don’t want to lose any data.

You can try this sequence as a starting point, but of course, depending on the project, you might need to change the wording a little bit.

## Prompt 1: give Claude a quick background and get an overview

Hey, I'm working on a project and need your help understanding this Excel file before we do anything to it. It's from a client who works in *\[industry / type of business, e.g. "a small retail store" or "an events company"\]*. They gave it to me to *\[general task, e.g. "clean up and organize" or "fix some errors and add calculations"\]*. Can you open it and tell me what you see; what's in there, how many sheets, what kind of data, anything that stands out?

### Prompt 2: Go deeper into the data

Now can you help me understand the data better? Like what does each column actually mean in plain English? What's this file being used for, do you think? And is there anything that looks off, like blank cells, weird formatting, duplicate rows, or columns that don't seem to match each other?

### Prompt 3: Turn the project into a real tasks list

Okay so here's what the client asked me to do: " *\[paste the client's message or describe what they want, e.g. "they want the sales data organized by month and a total at the bottom" or "they said there are errors in the formulas and they need a summary tab added"\]* ". Based on what you saw in the file, can you break this down into specific tasks? Like, what does this actually mean in terms of changes to the spreadsheet?

### Prompt 4: Confirm the plan before editing

Before we start changing anything, can you give me a clear summary of exactly what we're going to do? Just list the tasks in order, and flag if anything seems risky or might break something, or if there's anything you'd need me to clarify first.

### Prompt 5: Do the work one task at a time

Alright, let's start. Let's do task 1 first: *\[paste the first task from the plan, e.g. "remove all the duplicate rows in the Orders sheet"\]*. Walk me through what you're doing as you go so I understand it

### Prompt 5: Final check before sending it back

We've finished all the tasks. Can you do a final check on the file? Go through what we changed and make sure everything looks right and there are no broken formulas, no weird formatting leftover, and everything the client asked for is actually in there. Also let me know if you spot anything that might look unprofessional or that the client might question.
